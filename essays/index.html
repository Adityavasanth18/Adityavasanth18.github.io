About the Human Behind This

Hi, I'm Aditya. I'm supposed to be thinking about algorithms.

I'm a software engineer with a master's in Computer Science from the University of Maryland, Baltimore County — which means I spend my days doing things like optimizing gradient descent and my nights wondering whether the entity doing the optimizing has genuine phenomenal experience or is just very sophisticated symbol manipulation. Both questions keep me up equally.

My real obsession, the one I can't shake no matter how many deadlines loom, is the intersection of ancient Indian philosophical thought and contemporary cognitive science. I'm the kind of person who reads the Mahabharata the way others read thrillers — in great hungry gulps, completely unable to stop. The epic is, among other things, a 100,000-verse meditation on consciousness, moral catastrophe, and the impossibility of clean choices. It's also the most entertaining thing ever written. Fight me.

I'm particularly obsessed with Sanskrit literature and Vedantic philosophy — Śaṅkara's Advaita, the Nyāya logicians, Kashmir Śaivism. I find that these traditions have been thinking rigorously about the nature of mind and consciousness for two millennia, and it's a minor scandal that most Western philosophy of mind departments have never heard of the sākṣin or the spanda doctrine.

This blog is my attempt to think out loud. Some of it is scholarly (ish). Some of it is personal. All of it is sincere. Caveat lector.
